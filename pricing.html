FAQ

InstiQ pricing questions

How is the InstiQ price worked out?

You pay per student, each month: ₹10 for each of the first 500 students, ₹5 each for students 501–1,000, and ₹3 each above 1,000. So 500 students is ₹5,000, 1,000 is ₹7,500 and 2,000 is ₹10,500 a month. Teachers, staff and parents are free.

Are there setup fees or hidden charges?

No. Prices include all taxes and there is no setup fee. Our team gives free guided setup on a short call or screen share, and your data stays with you.

What do the add-ons cost?

Transport (buses, routes, drivers and bus alerts) is included at no extra charge. Video Lectures is ₹899 a month plus usage credit (from ₹100): storage ₹3 per GB a month and streaming ₹1 per GB.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days with every feature.
