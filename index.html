FAQ

Questions we hear often

What does InstiQ do?

InstiQ runs a school, coaching institute or college in one app: attendance, class diary and homework, exams and results, fee records, front desk inquiries, transport, video lectures, chat and messages, leave and parent-teacher meetings.

How much does it cost?

₹1,499 a month for up to 150 students, and ₹6 a month for each extra student. Teachers, staff and parents are free. Prices include all taxes, and there is a 14-day free trial.

Can parents use it in Hindi?

Yes. Parents switch the whole app between English and Hindi with one tap, and every video guide comes in both languages.

Do you help us set it up?

Yes. Send us your existing Excel lists of classes, teachers and students and we set up your institute for you.

Where does it work?

On Android phones (Google Play) and in any web browser at app.instiq.in.

Can you build a custom app for my business?

Yes. InstiQ Solutions builds Android apps, web apps and websites using AI-assisted development, which keeps projects fast and cost-effective. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss your idea.
