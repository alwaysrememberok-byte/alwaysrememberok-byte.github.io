FAQ

Questions, answered

What does "end-to-end" include?

Everything: the first discussion, research and market study, design, development, testing, publishing on Google Play, the App Store and the web, and support after launch. You deal with one team from idea to live product.

How quickly can you build my app?

Because we build with AI, you see working screens within days. The full timeline depends on your features — we give you a clear plan and dates after a free consultation.

How much does an app cost?

Every project is quoted to your needs. AI-powered development removes a lot of repetitive work, so our prices are usually lower than traditional development. Share your idea and we'll send a free estimate.

Do you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build Android and iOS apps from one modern codebase, plus web portals that work in any browser.

Can you build a custom ERP for my business?

Yes. We design ERP systems around how your business actually works — inventory, staff, billing, approvals and reports — so you don't pay for features you never use.

Can my app be in Hindi?

Yes. We build apps in English, Hindi or both, with a one-tap language switch for your users.

Do you support the app after launch?

Yes. We publish your app on Google Play and the App Store, and offer ongoing updates, support and new features.
