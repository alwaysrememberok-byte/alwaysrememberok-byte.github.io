What's inside

Every feature, live today

One school ERP for India's schools, coaching classes and colleges. Every feature below is included for every institute.

✅

Student attendance app

Teachers mark attendance class-wise or batch-wise and subject-wise in a few taps: Present, Absent or Late. Reports by class, section and date, with Student IDs.

👨‍🏫

Staff attendance & leave

Daily staff attendance (Present, Absent, Half day, Leave). Teachers apply for leave in the app, admins approve with leave rules and see which classes need a substitute.

📝

Exams & results

Plan exams per class, section or batch with total and passing marks. Teachers enter marks; students and parents see results and grades on their phone.

📚

Assignments

Set assignments with due dates. Students submit photos or files; teachers give feedback and grades.

📔

Class diary

Teachers post today's topic, class work and homework in seconds. Parents see what was taught every day.

🔔

Notices & notifications

Push notices to parents, teachers and/or students. Exam changes and cancelled events reach parents at once.

📅

Holidays & events

A holidays calendar, and events that show who is coming.

🛎️

Front desk (inquiries)

Log walk-ins and inquiries, give fee quotes per batch, see follow-ups due and overdue, reactivate old inquiries and convert an inquiry into an admission.

🗂️

Fees records

Set fees per batch or section and keep fee records for every student.

👨‍👩‍👧

School app for parents

One parent account for several children: attendance, results, assignments, class diary and notices.

📥

Bulk upload from Excel

Add a whole batch of students (parent accounts linked automatically), teachers, classes, subjects and faculty allocation from one sheet, with checks before anything is saved.

🕘

Faculty allocation

Assign teachers to subjects and time slots. Each teacher sees today's timetable.

🔄

Multi-institute

One person can belong to several institutes and switch in one tap. An owner can run several institutes.

🚌

Transport

Buses, routes, stops, drivers and riders.

✉️

Messages

Staff, students and parents write to the institute; the institute writes to InstiQ support.

🎬

Video Lectures Paid add-on

Share recorded lectures with the right batch only. The student's name moves over the video, screenshots and screen recording are blocked on Android, and it plays on one device at a time.

🔒

Security & privacy

Email verification for new accounts, a Student ID on every student record, delete-my-account inside the app, and data kept per institute.

FAQ

Questions about trial and pricing

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get 14 days with every feature, and no card is needed. After the trial, pay inside the app to continue. Your data stays safe.

How is the price worked out?

₹1,499 a month covers up to 150 students. Each student above 150 costs ₹6 a month. Teachers, staff and parents are free. For example, 300 students = ₹1,499 + 150 × ₹6 = ₹2,399 a month.

Can I change the number of students later?

Yes. Change it any time from Manage Subscription in the InstiQ app.

Is there a setup fee or any hidden charge?

No. There is no setup fee and no hidden charge. Prices include all taxes.

How do I pay?

Inside the InstiQ app (Manage Subscription) through Razorpay, using UPI, a debit or credit card, or net banking.
