What is InstiQ? For coaching institutes

Coaching class software built around courses and batches

Register as a coaching institute and InstiQ organises everything by courses and batches — attendance, tests, homework and notices all follow your batches. InstiQ runs on Android (Google Play) and on the web at app.instiq.in, and it is made in India, for Indian institutes.

Front desk: from inquiry to admission

Your receptionist gets a front desk built for coaching institutes:

  • Log walk-ins and inquiries in one place
  • Give fee quotes per batch
  • See follow-ups that are due and overdue
  • Reactivate old inquiries
  • Convert an inquiry into an admission

Set fees per batch and keep fee records for every student.

Tests, homework and attendance

Tests and results

Plan tests per batch with total and passing marks. Teachers enter marks, and students and parents see results and grades on their phone.

Homework and assignments

Set assignments with due dates. Students submit photos or files; teachers give feedback and grades. The class diary shows today's topic, class work and homework.

Batch-wise attendance

Teachers mark attendance batch-wise and subject-wise in a few taps — Present, Absent or Late — with reports by date.

Video Lectures (paid add-on)

Share recorded lectures with the right batch only. The student's name moves over the video, screenshots and screen recording are blocked on Android, and a lecture plays on one device at a time.

Keep students and parents informed

Push notices to students, parents and/or teachers, so exam changes and cancelled events reach them at once. Parents can follow several children with one account. Students and parents can also write to the institute through Messages.

One owner, several centres

An owner can run several institutes, and one person can belong to several institutes and switch in one tap. Teachers see today's timetable from faculty allocation, and staff attendance and leave are handled in the app.

Easy to start

Add a whole batch of students from one Excel sheet, with parent accounts linked automatically and checks before anything is saved. 26 short video guides inside the app explain every role in English and Hindi (कोचिंग मैनेजमेंट ऐप हिंदी में भी सीखें). See all features on the InstiQ home page.
