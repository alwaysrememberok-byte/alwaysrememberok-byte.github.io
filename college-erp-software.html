FAQ

Questions principals ask

Does InstiQ handle semesters and multiple courses?

Yes. Courses, semesters and sections are set up your way, and every screen uses those words.

How much does InstiQ cost?

You pay per student, each month: ₹10 for each of the first 500 students, ₹5 each for 501–1,000 and ₹3 each above 1,000 (500 students = ₹5,000 a month). Teachers, staff and parents are free, Transport is included, no setup fee, taxes included, and a 14-day free trial with every feature.

How long does setup take, and who does it?

Most institutes are running within a day or two. Our team gives free, step-by-step setup support on a short call or screen share while you add your classes, teachers and students — with ready Excel templates for bulk upload. Your data stays with you.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Your institute's data is kept confidential and stored securely. We never sell it or share it with anyone for their own use, and we follow India's data protection law (DPDP Act, 2023). Only your own staff, students and parents see what their role allows.

Does it work on mobile and computer?

Yes — Android app on Google Play and a web app at app.instiq.in for the office computer. An iPhone app is coming.
