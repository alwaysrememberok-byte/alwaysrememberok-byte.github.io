What is InstiQ? For colleges

An academic ERP shaped like your college

Register as a college and InstiQ sets up courses, semesters and subjects, and uses those words throughout the app. Owners, academic admins, faculty, students, parents and front desk staff each have their own screens. InstiQ works on Android (Google Play) and in the browser at app.instiq.in, and it is made in India, for Indian institutes.

Set up a whole semester from Excel

Bulk upload students, teachers, courses and subjects, and faculty allocation from one Excel sheet. Parent accounts are linked to students automatically, and InstiQ checks the sheet before anything is saved, so mistakes are caught first.

Faculty allocation and timetable

Assign faculty to subjects and time slots. Each teacher sees today's timetable as soon as they open the app.

Faculty attendance and leave

  • Daily staff attendance: Present, Absent, Half day or Leave
  • Faculty apply for leave in the app
  • Admins approve leave with leave rules
  • The admin sees which classes need a substitute

Attendance, results and assignments

Subject-wise attendance

Faculty mark student attendance subject-wise in a few taps — Present, Absent or Late — with attendance reports and a Student ID on every record.

Internal exams and results

Plan exams with total and passing marks. Faculty enter marks, and students and parents see results and grades on their phone.

Assignments

Set assignments with due dates. Students submit photos or files, and faculty give feedback and grades.

Notices, events and messages

Push notices to students, parents and/or faculty. Keep a holidays calendar and run events that show who is coming. Students, parents and staff can write to the college through Messages.

Fees records and privacy

Set fees and keep fee records. New accounts are verified by email, data is kept per institute, and users can delete their account inside the app. Recorded Video Lectures are available as a paid add-on. See every feature on the InstiQ home page.

Web app

On the computer too

Use InstiQ in the browser at app.instiq.in as well as on your Android phone.
