Education · Our own product

InstiQ — one app to run an entire institute

Schools, coaching institutes and colleges were juggling registers, spreadsheets and phone calls. We built one platform that puts every job — and every family — in one place.

THE CHALLENGE

Too many tools, too little time

Attendance on paper, homework on WhatsApp groups, fees in registers, parents always calling the office.

WHAT WE BUILT

A complete institute platform

Eight roles — owner, admin, teacher, student, parent, front desk, transport manager and driver — each with their own screens.

THE OUTCOME

Live on Google Play and the web

Teachers mark a class in a few taps, parents follow their child's day in Hindi or English, and owners see everything at a glance.

Field services · Client project

Doorstep vehicle services app

A service business wanted to bring two-wheeler servicing to the customer's doorstep — and run customers, mechanics and operations from one system.

THE CHALLENGE

Bookings on calls, jobs on paper

No single view of requests, mechanics, bills and payouts — and customers left guessing about status.

WHAT WE BUILT

Customer, partner and admin apps

Customers book with photos, voice notes or video; partners get jobs with one-tap call and navigate; admins assign and track everything.

THE OUTCOME

One live view of the business

Colour-coded status for every request, itemised bills from a service catalogue, partner earnings and revenue on one dashboard.

Social · Client project

Private dating & social app

A privacy-first app where adults meet like-minded people discreetly — built around trust, safety and real-time conversation.

THE CHALLENGE

Connection without exposure

Members wanted to meet people with shared interests without revealing their identity too early.

WHAT WE BUILT

Privacy-first matching and chat

Alias profiles, interest-based matching, private chat with voice notes, view-once photos and in-app voice and video calls.

THE OUTCOME

Safe, moderated community

Block and report, a full moderation panel, subscriptions and profile boosts — ready for growth.
