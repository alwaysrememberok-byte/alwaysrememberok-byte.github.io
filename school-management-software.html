What is InstiQ? For schools

A school ERP that follows your classes and sections

When you register your institute as a school, InstiQ sets itself up around classes and sections, and uses those words on every screen. The owner (Super Admin), academic admin, teachers, students, parents, front desk, transport manager and drivers each get their own screens, so everyone sees only what they need.

InstiQ is a mobile app on Android (Google Play) and a web app at app.instiq.in. It is made in India, for Indian institutes. The app is in English, and the in-app video guides are in English and Hindi.

Daily school work, done in the app

Student attendance app

Teachers mark attendance class-wise and subject-wise in a few taps — Present, Absent or Late. Reports are available by class, section and date, and every student record carries a Student ID.

Class diary for parents

Teachers post today's topic, class work and homework in seconds. Parents see what was taught every day, without chasing chat groups.

Exams and results

Plan exams per class and section with total and passing marks. Teachers enter marks, and students and parents see results and grades on their phone.

Assignments

Set assignments with due dates. Students submit photos or files, and teachers give feedback and grades.

Notices, holidays and events

Push notices to parents, teachers and/or students. Exam changes and cancelled events reach parents at once. A holidays calendar and events with “who is coming” keep everyone on the same page.

A school app for parents

One parent account covers several children. Parents see attendance, results, assignments, the class diary and notices, and can write to the school through Messages.

Staff, timetable and transport

  • Daily staff attendance: Present, Absent, Half day or Leave
  • Teachers apply for leave in the app; admins approve with leave rules and see which classes need a substitute
  • Faculty allocation: assign teachers to subjects and time slots, and each teacher sees today's timetable
  • Transport: buses, routes, stops, drivers and riders
  • Fees: set fees per class and section and keep fee records

Quick to set up

Add a whole class of students from one Excel sheet — parent accounts are linked automatically — along with teachers, classes, subjects and faculty allocation. InstiQ checks the sheet before anything is saved. Short video guides inside the app show every role how to get started, in English and Hindi (स्कूल मैनेजमेंट ऐप चलाना हिंदी में भी सीखें).

Your data is kept per institute, new accounts are verified by email, and users can delete their account inside the app. Want to see everything InstiQ does? Visit the InstiQ home page.
